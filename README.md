# Extra Ship Components NEXT

A fork of [Extra Ship Components NEXT](https://github.com/Solovyev-Pavel/nh_esc_next) by Solovyev-Pavel, aiming to fold quality improvements back upstream.

Available on the [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2648658105).

## About This Fork

This fork aims to contribute quality improvements back to the upstream project, organized in three stages:

1. **Typographical and grammar corrections** — fixing spelling errors and aligning English localisation text with vanilla Stellaris writing style.
2. **Cohesion and scientific consistency pass** — ensuring consistent terminology, verifying technology prerequisite chains, and reviewing category assignments.
3. **Technology additions and balance** — scope to be determined after stages 1 and 2 are complete.

## Mod Details

- **Current version:** 4.6.0
- **Supported Stellaris version:** v4.3.*
